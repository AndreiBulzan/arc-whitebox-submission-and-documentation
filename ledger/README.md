# Experiment ledger

This release preserves two generations of research records.

`legacy/` contains the early, continuous experiment ledger and its progress
summary. Agent-specific workspace labels were removed mechanically; the
experimental prose and numbers were otherwise retained.

`experiments/` contains 581 later preregistration and verdict documents,
preserving their original branch grouping. These are the compact records that
survived the public-export scan. Fifty-eight candidate records were omitted
because they contained local paths, agent-specific labels, authorization
notes, or another privacy-scan trigger. No attempt was made to repair them
under deadline pressure.

The ledger includes failed ideas. A preregistration is a proposed test, not a
claim that the mechanism worked. Prefer the paired verdict when one exists and
retain the evidence label attached to every numerical claim.
