## Question

Can the dominant K146 activation-cloud contractions be replaced by a
low-rank neuron-coordinate subspace without perturbing the final prediction
more than the estimator's existing error?

For every intercepted hidden contraction `H @ W` at layers 12--21, form the
best rank-`r` right subspace of the *current candidate state*:

```text
V_r = top-r eigenspace of H.T @ H
H @ W  ->  (H @ V_r) @ (V_r.T @ W)
```

This deliberately uses an oracle eigenspace and charges no deployability
claim. It is a strict capacity falsifier: if the best current-state
subspace fails, analytic, randomized, or learned subspaces at the same rank
cannot rescue this representation.

The first smoke row is sealed Full source row 0. Test ranks 24, 32, 48, 64,
and 96 independently. The exact dense hook result is the numerical control.
