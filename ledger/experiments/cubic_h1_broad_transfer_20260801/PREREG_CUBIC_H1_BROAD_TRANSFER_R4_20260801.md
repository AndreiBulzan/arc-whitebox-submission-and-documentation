# Cubic repaired-H1 broad transfer R4

Date: 2026-08-01

Evidence sought: **broad statistical component** evidence on the already
fixed Full100 and Generated128 rows used by the standard complete-K129
endpoint capture.  This is not a physical FlopScope row, packaged estimator,
or remote result.

## Fixed candidate

The pilot-selected rule is frozen before this capture:

- standard complete K129 primary frame;
- repaired-H1 cubic controls from the leading eight final-preactivation
  pullback directions;
- each direction sparsified to its eight largest H1 coordinates with the
  deterministic sign convention in the external exact-hinge implementation;
- exact spherical expectation from the proved three-ReLU hinge formula;
- even/odd half-frame minimum-norm calibration, `eta = 1`;
- average the two calibrated endpoint predictions;
- no shrinker, router, target fit, family-specific coefficient, or row-wise
  selection.

The Full100 and Generated128 per-frame endpoints were sealed previously and
remain immutable.  Capture opens weights and endpoint row identifiers only.
Targets are opened by a separate scorer after the controls are sealed.

## Gate

Promote to implementation costing only if, separately on Full100 and
Generated128:

- pooled raw-MSE ratio to the matching uniform K129 endpoint is at most
  `0.91`;
- at least 55% of networks improve;
- median row ratio is below `1.0`;
- nonparametric network-bootstrap p95 for the pooled ratio is at most `0.95`;
- all predictions and weights are finite.

This threshold leaves only about 1--2% of R27 effective compute for the
control implementation if the combined submission is to project at or below
`1.1e-7`.  Therefore passing accuracy is necessary but not sufficient.
