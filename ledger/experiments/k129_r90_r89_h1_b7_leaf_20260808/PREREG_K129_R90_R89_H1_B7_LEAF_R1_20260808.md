## Purpose

chassis.  Four repaired-H1 products currently use two B7 levels.  R90 adds a
third exact B7 level while preserving the estimator statistic, operands,
samples, repairs, Tichavsky R40 schedule, persistent bind views, and readout.

This is a residual engineering experiment, not a new estimator claim.  The
static saving ceiling is only about `0.155B` counted operations.  It is worth
testing because it is general, stackable, and already had a frozen dormant
implementation; it is not priced as a route to `1.10e-7` by itself.
