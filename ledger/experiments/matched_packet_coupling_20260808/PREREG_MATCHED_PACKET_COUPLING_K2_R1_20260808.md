# Matched packet coupling K2 R1 — preregistration

Date: 2026-08-08

Evidence scope: target-free **component** capture followed by a separately
sealed post-target **component** score.  No FlopScope session, physical row,
package, upload, submission, remote action, or production-score claim is
authorized.

## Question

Can cross-centre antithetic Gaussian packet coupling retain at least 70% of
the already measured integral-preserving packet gain while propagating only
one antipodal packet pair per Kerdock line?

For each unoriented Kerdock line `i`, define the pair response

```text
G_i(z) = 0.5 * [h(rho*u_i + tau*z) + h(-rho*u_i - tau*z)].
```

For every matched pair `(i,j)`, assign the common treatment `z` to one line
and `-z` to the other.  Each line's marginal packet noise remains exactly
`N(0,I)`.  Conditional on the finite packet pool, randomizing the within-pair
assignment is exactly unbiased.

Outcome: **materially new observable**.  This attempt changes the joint law
of actual packet evaluations while retaining every centre's exact Gaussian
marginal.  It does not predict a residual, compress a state, or continue the
finite network with a GP.

## Target ceiling

Existing sealed receipts give approximately 53.57% true-packet raw-MSE
reduction and 96.10% retention by the finite `m=8` packet pool.  At the public
R87 scale, retaining 70% of the packet gain permits about `3.11e-8` additional
per-output coupling error above that pool.  This is enough headroom for a
20%+ raw improvement at one-frame compute if the coupling works.

No public-scale score is inferred by this oracle.  The primary gate is
defined on the exact four selected local rows and their sealed packet truth.

## Teacher matching

For three global probe directions `z_r`, evaluate both `G_i(z_r)` and
`G_i(-z_r)` for every line.  Form the concatenated contrast

```text
x_i = concat_r [G_i(z_r) - G_i(-z_r)] in R^(3*256).
```

Line orientation is free.  Pair cost is the exact projective distance

```text
d(i,j)^2 = min(||x_i-x_j||^2, ||x_i+x_j||^2).
```

The frozen teacher solver uses exact blockwise projective K-nearest-neighbour
edges, greedy perfect matching, recursive unmatched repair, and fixed-seed
two-pair exchange improvements.  It records its objective relative to fixed
random and natural within-basis pairings.  This is an intentionally
overpowered, unavailable-output capacity oracle.

## Cross-treatment evaluation

Training probe treatments are never reused for evaluation.  For every
matched pair and each of four evaluation replicates, draw a new independent
group-specific `z`.  Evaluate the complete `2 x 2` table

```text
G_i(+z), G_i(-z)
G_j(+z), G_j(-z)
```

after applying the teacher orientation.  Let `pool` be the average of all
four entries.  If

```text
delta = [G_i(+z)-G_i(-z)] - [G_j(+z)-G_j(-z)],
```

then the exact average per-output assignment variance of the complete
66,048-row estimator is

```text
sum_pairs ||delta||^2 / (4 * M^2 * 256),  M=33,024.
```

The post-seal expected raw MSE is exactly

```text
MSE(pool, target) + assignment_variance,
```

averaged over the four independent evaluation treatment sets.

## Primary gates

Let `q0` be canonical K129 error and `ideal` the sealed unbiased packet error.
For a method error `candidate`, define packet-gain retention as

```text
(q0 - candidate) / (q0 - ideal).
```

Continue to `k=8` and accessible matching only if all hold:

1. pooled expected packet-gain retention is at least `0.70`;
2. Full expected retention is at least `0.60`;
3. Generated expected retention is at least `0.60`;
4. at least three of four rowwise expected errors improve on q0;
5. all outputs and variances are finite and the randomized-draw mean agrees
   with the exact assignment formula within its reported Monte Carlo error.

Failure kills this teacher-matched `k=2` spelling.  It closes the broader
local-matching family only when the teacher solver materially beats both
random and natural pairing on its frozen training objective yet still fails
cross-treatment.  Otherwise the result is solver-inconclusive.

Passing licenses only a target-free `k=8` common-treatment oracle and cheap
accessible-matching tests.  It does not license estimator implementation,
compute pricing, packaging, upload, submission, or a remote-score claim.
