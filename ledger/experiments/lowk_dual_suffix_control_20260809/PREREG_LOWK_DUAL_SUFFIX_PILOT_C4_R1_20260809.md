# Low-K dual exact suffix-pilot control C4 R1

Date: 2026-08-09

Evidence sought: target-free CUDA component capture on fixed public16,
followed by post-seal replay with the sealed C2 literal per-basis endpoints.
No Mini100, physical, package, upload, or remote action.

C2 proves 14-tail target-aware capacity (`4.465e-7`). C3 proves that complete
degree-two checkpoint moments still do not identify that support. The next
observable is therefore a small number of actual deep trajectories from every
basis, not another checkpoint surrogate.

For each of the 20 ordinary and 20 polar bases, propagate nested sets of
`2,4,8,16,32,64` antipodal coordinate pairs through the actual suffix. Capture
two deterministic within-basis orders:

1. the canonical coordinate order;
2. a target-free layer-5 pair-moment herding order.

Pilot endpoint corrections must be computed only from the observed pilot
preactivations, never from the unobserved full basis. The all-basis full tails
are evaluated only in this component capture so that the residual-control
curve can be replayed exactly.

For each pilot size and order, test:

- direct sparse exact tails selected by the pilot endpoint kernel;
- pilot-population plus a weighted exact-minus-pilot residual on those tails;
- the same residual control on the frozen C3/D3 support;
- fixed-prefix residual control.

All support and weights are target-free, nonnegative, arm-balanced, and capped
as in C2/C3. The production-equivalent tail count is reported as

`2*m + (40-2*m)*r/256`,

because pilot rows on selected bases can be reused by their complete tails.

Primary gate: raw MSE at most `1.0e-6` with at most seven complete bases per
arm and at most 16 pilot pairs per basis. Preferred gate: raw MSE at most
`8.0e-7` within a projected 27.2B total. Larger pilots are diagnostic only.
Failure closes this exact suffix-pilot residual control, not a learned outcome
model or a different estimator family.
