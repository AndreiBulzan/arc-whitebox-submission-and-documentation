not change C2's literal 14-tail target-aware capacity (`4.4647e-7`), but it
makes a deployable bridge substantially less likely: neither complete
checkpoint covariance nor small exact suffix samples reveal the useful
support. Reopen only with a deterministic contraction identity, a learned
per-network support model that passes held-family gates, or a different
estimator class.
