# Preregistration: R92 = exact R90 plus fixed BF-XCORR

Date: 2026-08-09

## Frozen estimator change

R92 inherits the complete sealed R90 execution graph.  It changes only the
final O0 ReLU readout.  The 129 bases are assigned to the already-sealed
two-fold partition generated by seed `2026080901`.  Each fold receives a
signed-preactivation correction computed from the complementary fold, and the
two corrections are recentered so their basis-weighted mean remains the
ordinary global correction.

Frozen constants:

- fold count: `2`;
- fold seed: `2026080901`;
- correction lambda: `0.015`;
- basis counts: `65, 64`;
- endpoint scale and every propagated trajectory: inherited unchanged from
  R90.

This is the exact cell selected on official Mini100 rows 0--49 after a
target-free grid seal.  It improved the untouched rows 50--99 by `0.945664%`,
the public half by `1.206169%`, and pooled Mini100 by `1.060627%`.  The rule
contains no row IDs, targets, fitted network weights, or router.

## Evidence boundary and gates

1. Build a deterministic archive from the exact R90 archive and pin every
   source and archive SHA.
2. Run at least one initialized and one steady official-runner row, requiring
   output association to the already-sealed target-free BF-XCORR Mini100
   prediction within `2e-7` maximum absolute error and `1e-14` mean squared
   association error.
3. Run the exact archive on all 100 official Mini100 networks target-free,
   seal outputs, then score.  Require both halves to remain improved and the
   pooled raw reduction to retain at least 80% of the offline `1.060627%`
   reduction.
4. Require the paired bootstrap probability of pooled raw improvement to be
   reported.  This small candidate is bankable as a deadline-safe descendant
   only if the central exact-package score improves R90; uncertainty is
   reported rather than hidden.
5. On a quiet isolated ABBA, require added effective compute below `0.15B` per
   row and no wall/cap/protocol regression.

Evidence labels remain `component`, `measured whole`, `broad statistical`,
and `projection` at their exact scopes.  No upload or submission is
authorized.

## Reopen rationale

The earlier verdict called BF-XCORR "killed" because it missed a deliberately
large 20% research gate.  It did not fail transfer: the public-selected fixed
cell improved both Mini100 halves.  The deadline objective now explicitly
values general 1--2% gains, so this is promotion of an already-frozen positive
cell, not a new tune or a relaxation after seeing a new target bank.
