# K129 dynamic cloud compression R1 — hard kill

Date: 2026-07-29.

Evidence label: the accuracy screens are **component development
diagnostics**. All operation-count and adjusted-score arithmetic is a
**projection**. No FlopScope session, physical row, benchmark-lane entry,
estimator edit, package, upload, submission, or remote action occurred.

## Verdict

**Kill the tested activation-aware K129 compression mechanisms and the
tested low-fidelity O1[70,45] sidecars.** None is a credible composition for
the `1.2e-7` checkpoint.

## Strongest dynamic-compression result

The strongest spelling propagated all 129 O0 bases to L16, used the four
already-frozen target-free output-response coordinates to choose a
per-network omitted support, and then calibrated the retained basis weights
to reproduce the complete L16 response mean exactly. Only the retained bases
were charged for the nonlinear suffix.

| retained bases | Full raw ratio | Synthetic raw ratio | optimistic saving | Full raw×compute | Synthetic raw×compute |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 120 | `1.020486` | `1.025102` | `4.246B` | `0.991025` | `0.995508` |
| 112 | `1.075583` | `1.069957` | `8.021B` | `1.016929` | `1.011610` |
| 96 | `1.189754` | `1.187622` | `15.569B` | `1.063811` | `1.061904` |

The economic calculation is deliberately favorable: it prices each omitted
basis using the observed full-depth K129/K146 slope, charges only `15/31` of
that slope after L16, and ignores selection, calibration, requests, and
residual entirely. Even then the best product gain is below one percent,
versus the predeclared three-percent minimum. It does not close the remote
checkpoint gap.

Coefficient-one response control variates, within-MLP GREG/kernel maps, and
activation-nearest-pair weight transfer were all weaker. The nearest-pair
idea, for example, reached only `1.10716x / 1.09305x` raw at 120 retained
bases.

## Low-fidelity nonlinear O1 sidecar

The exact O1[70,45] correction remains a real accuracy component:
`0.982808x` Full and `0.984234x` Synthetic. Its `1.9501B` affine count,
however, leaves only a `0.99584 / 0.99728` count-only score product before
request residual.

Two cheaper attempts fail:

- replacing each O1 endpoint by its already-paid same-label O0 nonlinear
  endpoint is zero-cost but worsens raw to `1.02873x / 1.02939x`;
- propagating the actual O1 nonlinear state to L8, then predicting its
  endpoint from the four nearest paid O0 nonlinear endpoints in
  output-response space, gives `1.001335x` Full before charging its optimistic
  `0.5033B` prefix. Its raw×compute product is `1.004761`, already negative.

Thus the L8 proxy retains some correlation with the exact O1 endpoint but
not its small target-aligned component. There is no reason to buy a current
source capture or physical price for it.

## Scope of the kill

This closes the exact tested class:

- basis pruning after L16 using response-moment herding/calibration;
- coefficient-one or within-MLP regression controls on those same frozen
  observables;
- nearest-pair weight transfer from basis-mean activation geometry; and
- O1[70,45] replacement by same-label O0 or L8-to-O0 endpoint surrogates.

It does not claim that every conceivable state compression is impossible.
A reopening needs a new observable that predicts the target-aligned
nonlinear suffix innovation, not another mean, affine response, endpoint
neighbor, or calibration of the four L16 coordinates.

in `k129_dynamic_cloud_compression_r1_static_20260729.json`.
