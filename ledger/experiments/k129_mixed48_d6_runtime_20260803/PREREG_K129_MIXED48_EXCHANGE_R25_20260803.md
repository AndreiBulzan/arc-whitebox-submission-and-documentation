# K129 mixed48 target-free support exchange — R25 preregistration

Date: 2026-08-03

Evidence before execution: **component**.  Runtime and adjusted-score effects
are **projections** until a physical successor exists.

## Prior-art boundary

Searches covered `local swap`, `swap optimizer`, `support exchange`,
`backward elimination`, `forward OMP`, `48 of 64`, and `subset optimizer`.
R35's 48 atoms are the size-48 prefix of one forward OMP path.  The recorded
support work does not run backward elimination or one-for-one exchange on the
same sealed literal64/D6 feature bank and q0-core64 anchor.

## Fixed target-free procedure

Use exactly R8's sealed Full100/Generated128 feature and complete-four-frame
teacher, its four ordinal folds, family-balanced Gram/cross statistics, and
ridge-relative `1.0`.

For each training fold and once on all rows:

1. construct both the forward-OMP size-48 support and a backward-elimination
   size-48 support from all 64 atoms;
2. from each start, repeatedly apply the single remove/add exchange with the
   largest strict decrease in the regularized training objective;
3. stop at a one-exchange local optimum and retain the better of the two
   local optima, with lexicographic support as the deterministic tie break;
4. refit the 48 coefficients on that support using the unchanged ridge.

No challenge target, family-specific deployment rule, support size, ridge,
scale, direction count, frame, or runtime graph enters this procedure.

## Gate

Open the challenge targets only if the exchanged support's worst-family OOF
teacher reconstruction MSE is below the original OMP48 worst-family value.
Promote to constants/package work only if the post-seal adjusted projection
improves both families over R35.  Runtime is intended to remain identical to
R35; this must later be confirmed physically rather than assumed.
