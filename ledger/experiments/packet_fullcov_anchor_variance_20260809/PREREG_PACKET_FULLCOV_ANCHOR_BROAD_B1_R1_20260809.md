## Post-seal gates

The primary rule passes the scout if:

- corrected pooled raw-MSE ratio versus the sealed lambda=0.0075 baseline is
  at most `0.80`;
- each family ratio is at most `0.85`;
- at least half the rows improve in each family;
- all outputs are finite.

A near pass is pooled ratio at most `0.90` with neither family worse than
`0.95`. A strong pass licenses immediate Full100 plus Generated64 capture and
a production cost audit. Failure kills only this fixed two-anchor estimator,
not larger anchor sets or other self-consistent covariance summaries.
