# K129 full-17 O1 cheap-trajectory R1 — decisive kill

Date: 2026-07-29.

Evidence label: the accuracy results are **component development
diagnostics**.  Operation counts are **projections**.  The CUDA archives
were target-free and sealed before scoring.  No FlopScope session, physical
row, estimator edit, package, network action, upload, submission, or remote
action occurred.

## Bottom line

The exact full-17 O1 Edgeworth3433 statistic remains a real accuracy
component.  Neither tested way of transporting all 17 bases cheaply retains
its target-aligned innovation:

1. reducing the neuron axis to 48--96 target-free
   observability-ranked coordinates destroys the O1 delta; and
2. retaining the production neuron trajectory through layer 12, then
   thinning the 256 matched antipodal directions per basis to 64 or 128,
   also fails.

There is no candidate to integrate or broaden.

## Spatial-width shadow

Every shadow preserved all 17 bases, the two signs, the exact production O1
front/H2 repair, a reduced-width L4 moment snap, and the final 256-coordinate
Edgeworth readout.  Omitted neuron coordinates entered subsequent layers
through the existing analytic-mean fold.  Supports were fixed independently
per MLP from analytic post-ReLU variance times weight-derived downstream
observability.

At fixed `beta=.0734` and output scale `1.000025`:

| shadow schedule | post-front dense count projection | Full8 raw ratio | Generated8 corrected raw ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| width 64, L2--L30 | `2.5832B` | `10.0466` | `6.3636` |
| 48 / 64 / 96 at L2--15 / L16--23 / L24--30 | `2.8016B` | `14.1799` | `8.9003` |
| 64 / 80 / 96 at L2--15 / L16--23 / L24--30 | `3.6246B` | `16.3586` | `10.6504` |

Those counts exclude the unchanged exact O1 front, so they are not claimed
as total incremental counts.  The accuracy failure is decisive even if the
front were free.

This is not a numerical explosion.  The shadow Edgeworth readouts have
normal amplitude (`RMS .926--.991`), but their target-free RMSE from exact O1
is `.0179--.0255`, versus an exact O1-minus-O0 signal RMS of only
`.00159--.00194`.  Joint target-free shrinkage fitted on the first four rows
of each family gives held-family delta correlations near zero and sometimes
opposite sign.  The directed innovation is absent, not merely mis-scaled.

## Within-basis direction thinning

The distinct follow-up kept the complete production O1 neuron trajectory
through layer 12 and all 17 bases, then retained a deterministic
floor/ceil-balanced subset of matched positive/negative direction IDs inside
every basis.  The normal production suffix, energy selections, final-mean
fold, and raw-moment readout were unchanged.

| directions per basis after L12 | Full8 raw ratio | Generated8 corrected raw ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 64 | `1.16673` | `1.45060` |
| 128 | `1.00154` | `1.34491` |

Both miss the predeclared `<=.97` gate in both families.

They also miss the `<=4B` incremental-count ceiling before any residual is
charged.  Using the measured K146-minus-R21 steady count
`144,013,215,420 - 127,347,663,042 = 16.66555B` and granting the direction
thinner the full arm-proportional share of the prior ratified K146 savings
gives favorable lower-bound projections:

```text
m128 incremental count >= 12.121B
m64  incremental count >=  9.849B
```

The fixed through-L12 prefix alone prevents this spelling from becoming a
four-billion-operation sidecar.

## Decision

Kill the tested full-17 narrow-neuron and post-L12 direction-thinning
trajectories.  Do not scan nearby widths, layer-12 direction cardinalities,
support rankings, or scalar shrinkage.  A reopening needs a representation
that directly preserves the orientation-specific connected innovation; low
spatial dimension, uniform direction deletion, Gaussian mean folding, and
scalar calibration do not.
