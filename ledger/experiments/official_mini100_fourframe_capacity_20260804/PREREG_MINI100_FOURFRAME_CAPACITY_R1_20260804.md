# Official Mini100 complete-four-frame capacity R1

Date: 2026-08-04

This is a capacity experiment, not a package or adjusted-score claim.  Before
opening the official Mini100 targets, seal four complete K129 predictions for
every row:

1. the frozen q0 frame;
2. the canonical right-Gram frame of `W0`;
3. the depth-2 pullback frame from `J J^T`;
4. the depth-2 right-pullback frame from `J^T J`.

Every arm rotates the entire 129-basis spherical design.  No sparse mixing,
target fitting, or row selection is allowed.  The primary candidate is the
equal-weight mean of all four predictions.  The fixed secondary diagnostics
are q0 alone, the q0/right mean, and the q0/right/d2 mean.

The capture must be target-free, finite, and hash sealed before scoring.  The
post-seal report will give raw MSE, ratio to q0, paired bootstrap confidence
interval, and improved-row fraction.  A four-frame raw ratio at or below
`0.70` is a strong transferable-capacity result worth compressing; a ratio
above `0.85` closes this branch for near-term deployment.  Values in between
remain research evidence only.

Because four complete frames cost far more than the current package, no
result from this experiment is a remotely admissible candidate.  Evidence is
`broad statistical` capacity evidence on the fixed official Mini100 regression
bank.  It authorizes no physical run, upload, or submission.
