## One untested successor worth retaining

Mean folding discards all omitted fluctuations.  A genuinely different
successor is analytic conditional reconstruction:

```text
h_drop ~= mu_drop + (h_keep - mu_keep) B
B = Cov(keep,keep)^(-1) Cov(keep,drop)
```

The next right can then be replaced target-free by

```text
W_keep' = W_keep + B W_drop
fold'   = mu_drop W_drop - mu_keep B W_drop.
```

This is the optimal analytic linear imputation of omitted fluctuations and
keeps the sampled trajectory width unchanged.  It could, in principle,
recover the 13.6% Full raw loss at s12/w200 without restoring its 9.0B
counted sampled work.  It was not preregistered or tested in this lane and is
only a mechanism proposal, not evidence.
