# One-site cavity/TAP ReLU closure R1

Date: 2026-07-29

Evidence label: **component**. The target-free phase used two already-used
Full rows and two already-used Generated rows, then froze and hashed literal
predictions before a separate scorer opened those four development targets.
No FlopScope session, physical benchmark, package, upload, submission, or
remote action occurred.

## Decision

**Hard kill. There is no low-compute candidate from this recurrence.**

The one-site cavity correction was deliberately distinct from the killed
finite-order K3/K4 closures: it analytically resums every cumulant generated
by one rectified coordinate inside a moment-matched, weight-column-specific
Gaussian cavity. It has coefficient one from the Lindeberg replacement
identity and no target-fitted parameter.

It worsened every sealed row:

| family | Gaussian Price-8 raw MSE | cavity raw MSE | ratio | rows improved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full2 | `5.887087e-5` | `3.397354e-4` | `5.770858x` | `0/2` |
| Generated2 | `5.397247e-5` | `2.267995e-4` | `4.202132x` | `0/2` |
| pooled four | `5.642167e-5` | `2.832674e-4` | `5.020543x` | `0/4` |

The preregistered continuation gate was `<=0.10x` on both families because a
floor-compute analytic estimator must improve the roughly `6e-5` Gaussian
closure by orders of magnitude. R1 missed that gate by factors of
`42--58`, and missed the actual `~1e-7` raw objective by about three orders
of magnitude. No coefficient, Price order, quadrature order, layer window,
or clipping sweep follows.

## Concrete recurrence tested

At one layer let the Gaussian closure for the previous preactivations be

```text
U ~ N(m, C)
X_i = delta_i + ReLU(U_i)
E[X] = mu
Cov(X) = H
S_a = sum_i W_ia X_i
M_a = (mu W)_a
V_a = (W^T H W)_aa.
```

`delta_i` carries the preceding layer's cavity mean correction while keeping
the Price covariance central.

For site `i` and output column `a`, define the cavity

```text
R_ia = S_a - W_ia X_i

E[R_ia]   = M_a - W_ia mu_i
Var(R_ia) = V_a + W_ia^2 H_ii - 2 W_ia (H W)_ia
d_ia      = Cov(X_i, R_ia) = (H W)_ia - W_ia H_ii.
```

Approximate `(U_i,R_ia)` as jointly Gaussian but choose its covariance to
preserve the exact cavity cross moment:

```text
Cov(U_i,R_ia) = d_ia / Phi(m_i / sqrt(C_ii)),
```

using the Gaussian Stein identity

```text
Cov(ReLU(U_i),R_ia)
  = Phi(m_i/sqrt(C_ii)) Cov(U_i,R_ia).
```

The one-site nonlinear replacement is then

```text
A_ia = E ReLU(R_ia + W_ia[delta_i + ReLU(U_i)])
```

under that two-variable Gaussian. R1 evaluates the remaining one-dimensional
normal expectation with fixed 24-point Gauss--Hermite quadrature. The next
mean is the first-order cluster expansion

```text
mu'_a = g(M_a,V_a) + sum_i [A_ia - g(M_a,V_a)],

g(M,V) = E ReLU(N(M,V)),
```

followed by the parameter-free interval

```text
max(0,M_a) <= mu'_a <= sqrt(M_a^2 + V_a).
```

The covariance recurrence remains the order-eight Price/Hermite Gaussian
covariance. Layer zero is exact Gaussian; the cavity correction begins at
layer one and rolls through layer 31.

This is a finite-width/TAP correction: each bracket is a connected one-site
cluster beyond the moment-matched Gaussian, and their sum is first order in
the weak individual weight contributions. Unlike a K3/K4 Edgeworth
truncation, `A_ia` includes all one-site Hermite orders.

## Failure is structural, not a numerical no-op

The statistic moved predictions materially:

```text
final candidate-minus-Price RMS by row
Full       0.0154174, 0.00708518
Generated  0.00844735, 0.00973427
```

All four outputs were finite. No negative cavity variance occurred. Only
`110` correlation entries were clipped across roughly eight million
site/output/layer opportunities, so feasibility clipping cannot explain the
sign. The Jensen/RMS interval became active at some coordinates in
`29--30` layers per row, showing that the parameter-free first-order
clusters accumulate secularly rather than repairing the closure.

The final-layer-only cavity increments were much smaller
(`0.000338--0.000594` RMS); the large error is generated by rolling the
one-site correction through depth. That observation does not justify a
final-layer sweep: the exact-teacher leading-tree study already gave the
mean/covariance-only final transition its stronger capacity test and failed
the `1.2e-7` gate.

## Cost projection

Compute was not the blocker. A participant-oriented vectorization can form
all site/output/quadrature values for one layer as a
`(24,256,256)` float32 array:

```text
elements over 31 corrected layers          48,758,784
largest such ordinary result                 6,291,456 bytes
extra H @ W contractions, multiply-add      ~1.040 B
elementwise/quadrature work                 roughly 1--3 B
existing full-covariance closure anchor       1.310 B
```

Thus a careful graph plausibly lies in the low-single-digit billions of
counted work, with a conservative `~3--8B` total-count **projection** before
participant residual and exact FlopScope translation. It would be well below
the `27.2B` multiplier floor if the runtime request surface were also
controlled. This is not a FlopScope receipt. The accuracy failure makes
physical pricing moot.

## Exhaustion boundary

R1 closes the remaining simple member of the mean/covariance-only
finite-width family:

- pure Gaussian/Price closure is around `6e-5`;
- literal propagated SIMPLE K3 remains around `2.6e-5`;
- leading Gaussian/ReLU K3/K4 trees fail even with exact teacher state;
- final-hidden conditional Gaussianization fails;
- endpoint K4 refactorings do not repair the signed final mean;
- and now an all-orders, weight-conditioned one-site cavity correction
  worsens both process families by `4.2--5.8x`.

This is not a proof against every analytic estimator. It is a concrete
negative boundary: a viable finite-width method must carry a genuinely
multi-site/all-distinct connected state through depth. Mean, covariance,
single-site cumulants, and endpoint-only cumulant queries are insufficient.
That surviving requirement is the expensive state identified by the exact
K3+K4 oracle and the repeated-view/all-distinct studies; it is not available
from this recurrence.
