# Gate-boundary adaptive cubature R1 verdict

Date: 2026-07-29

## Verdict

**Killed quickly.**  Literal matched O0/O1 first-layer gate-cell innovations,
even after downstream Price-adjoint weighting, contain essentially zero
information about which O1 bases have small signed-final-preactivation
quadrature error.  Do not build this support router and do not scan alternate
H1 margins, kernels, whitening, or herding rules.

This is target-free **component** evidence on the frozen S109 endpoint proxy.
It is not a challenge score, physical receipt, package, or remote result.

## What was genuinely tested

For all 129 matched O0/O1 bases, R1 computed literal W0 preactivation signs,
signed gate crossings, crossing margins, ReLU-curvature differences, and
cell-disagreement rates.  Each neuron coordinate was weighted by a
weight-only reverse mean-square Price adjoint through W1..W31.  A fixed
17-step equal-weight herder matched the frozen S109 feature centroid.
Endpoints were opened only after each support was fixed.

The lawful arithmetic increment would have fit easily: at most a projected
`3.920B`, for projected total counted arithmetic `147.933B` from the remote
steady anchor.  That projection is moot because the statistic does not
predict the quadrature error.

## Boundary learned

First-layer gate geometry is not the missing state.  The frozen broad atlases
retain only per-basis post-ReLU means at L4/L8, not literal particle gate
cells, so they cannot test a genuinely deeper gate-boundary router.  Such a
test would require a new small-pilot capture at a late layer.  If reopened,
the only scientifically distinct version is a late (roughly L24--L30)
literal sign-crossing pilot weighted by the *actual remaining* readout
adjoint; endpoint or checkpoint-mean threshold proxies are not a substitute.
