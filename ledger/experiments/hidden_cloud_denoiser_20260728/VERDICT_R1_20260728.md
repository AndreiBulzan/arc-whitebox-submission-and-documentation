```text

equal-K32 signed MSE              2.913883e-6      2.848403e-6
```

The correction improved 48.0% of Full rows and 40.625% of Generated rows,
but worsened the pooled errors by 2.42% and 1.04%. It missed the required
gate by about 5.97x and 5.76x.

This closes the exact r1 hypothesis: retaining the final `256 x 32` hidden
basis-mean state, adding hidden-permutation-equivariant DeepSets context and
a `W31 W31.T` graph, and contracting the learned correction through the
literal `W31` does not recover the missing small-cloud signal.

The numerical recovery itself was not the failure. Solving
`endpoint_basis = hidden_basis_mean @ W31` reconstructed the saved endpoint
values to maximum absolute residual `6.73e-14` on Full and `5.57e-14` on
Generated. The selected inner-dev checkpoint was already slightly worse
than the equal-K32 control in both families, and later checkpoints overfit.

The useful boundary is causal: the final hidden basis means and the adjacent
readout geometry do not contain a stable, cross-network observable for the
omitted quadrature error at K32. Reopening this class needs a genuinely new
upstream observable—such as per-layer hidden innovations, gate/covariance
state, or checkpoint rejuvenation—not a wider endpoint/hidden-state head.

The inference economics remain only a projection (`~31.65B` before
residual). No FlopScope implementation, physical row, package, upload,
remote action, or submission was made.
